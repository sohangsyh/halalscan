HOW IT WORKS

      
    

    
    
      
  1. Point your camera at a product's ingredient list or the product/dish itself, or upload a photo.
    2. 
      
  2. On-device OCR (Tesseract.js) extracts any printed text automatically — no language selection needed.
    3. 
      
  3. An on-device image recognizer (MobileNet) also looks at the photo itself, to catch visual cues like alcohol bottles or pork-source animals when no ingredient text is present.
    4. 
      
  4. Both results are checked against a Halal / Haram / Mashbooh database covering common translations, E-numbers, derivatives, and visual cues.
    5. 
      
  5. The photo on screen tints green, red, or amber with an instant verdict.
    6. 
    

    
This tool provides guidance only and does not replace official Halal certification bodies. When in doubt, contact the manufacturer.
