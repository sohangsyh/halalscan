How it works

      
    

    
    
      
  1. Point your camera at a product's ingredient list, or upload a photo.
    2. 
      
  2. On-device OCR (Tesseract.js) extracts the text automatically — no language selection needed.
    3. 
      
  3. The extracted text is scanned against a Halal/Haram/Mashbooh ingredient database covering common translations, E-numbers, and derivatives.
